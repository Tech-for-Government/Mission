# State Powers

## Executive
* Creation and removal of ministries and agencies
* Funding of ministries

## Legislative
* Creation, change, deletion of:
  * Laws
  * Organic laws
  * Rights
  * Duties

## Justice
* Powers of court
  * Hierarchy of norms: positive redression
  * Definitional interpretation
  * Truth-seeking purpose: equity and objectivity
  * Police and enforcement powers

# **II. Legislative Powers**

---

## **Department of Legislative Drafting**

* Draft laws based on policy priorities
* Translate executive proposals into legal language
* Harmonize new laws with existing statutes
* Evaluate policy implications of laws
* Provide technical support to lawmakers

## **Department of Lawmaking Procedure**

* Schedule legislative debates
* Manage committee review process
* Organize public consultations
* Control amendment procedures
* Enforce legislative transparency rules

## **Department of Budget Oversight**

* Approve annual national budget
* Audit public expenditure reports
* Authorize new taxation measures
* Debate supplementary appropriations
* Set fiscal policy guidelines

## **Department of Representation**

* Elect legislative officers
* Maintain records of parliamentary sessions
* Communicate with constituents
* Register interest groups and lobbyists
* Manage inter-legislative coordination

# **III. Judicial Powers**

---

## **Supreme Court**

* Interpret constitutional provisions
* Rule on legality of laws
* Resolve jurisdictional disputes
* Hear appeals from lower courts
* Oversee judicial ethics

## **Court of Appeals**

* Hear cases on procedural errors
* Rule on matters of administrative law
* Review agency enforcement actions
* Issue legal precedents
* Monitor compliance with appellate decisions

## **Trial Courts**

**Criminal Division**

* Hear cases of theft, assault, and murder
* Issue arrest and search warrants
* Sentence convicts based on law
* Rule on bail and parole eligibility
* Oversee jury selection

**Civil Division**

* Adjudicate property and contract disputes
* Issue injunctions
* Rule on tort claims
* Enforce civil judgments
* Mediate small claims

**Family Division**

* Rule on custody and divorce
* Grant legal guardianship
* Handle adoption procedures
* Issue protective orders
* Manage family mediation services

## **Administrative Tribunal**

* Rule on disputes involving state agencies
* Hear complaints of government abuse
* Adjudicate public service terminations
* Enforce licensing decisions
* Interpret regulations
